↩️ Return Policy

At Rajvansh Ventures, we offer a 7-day hassle-free return policy on all products. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, we'll make it right.

  • Returns must be initiated within 7 days of delivery
  • Items must be in original condition with all packaging intact
  • Used, washed, or damaged items cannot be returned (unless defective)
  • Custom or personalised orders are non-returnable
  • Free pickup from your doorstep — no courier hassle for you

💰 Refund Policy

  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of receiving the returned item
  • Amount credited to your original payment method (card/bank/UPI)
  • COD refunds are transferred directly to your bank account
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable (unless item was defective)
