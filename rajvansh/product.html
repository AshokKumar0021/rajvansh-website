Our Royal Velvet Blackout Curtains are crafted from 100% premium velvet fabric with triple-weave blackout lining. They effectively block sunlight, reduce noise, and provide complete privacy.

Perfect for bedrooms, nurseries, and home theatres. Each set comes with 2 panels with eyelet rings for easy installation on standard curtain rods.

  • Material: 100% Premium Velvet with blackout lining
  • Blackout level: 99% light blocking
